CENTRAL QUESTION
----------------
How are safety-derived boundaries of local autonomy translated into formally
verifiable decision and escalation rules at the interface between Module
System Managers (MSMs) and the Vehicle System Manager (VSM) in NASA Gateway?

CENTRAL FINDING
---------------
NASA Gateway publicly documents the existence of safety-derived autonomous
operation requirements, distributed control responsibilities, formal
contract-based verification, and runtime verification. However, the reviewed
public documentation does not establish a complete traceability chain
connecting hazard-derived autonomy boundaries to specific MSM decision and
escalation rules and their corresponding verification properties.

SCOPE CAVEAT
------------
The finding concerns public documentation only. It makes no claim about
the absence of mechanisms in implementation.
