# Contributing to the NASA Gateway Research Project

## Status

This project is a **documentary analysis of publicly available NASA 
documentation**. The manuscript is at version 1.0.0 and the central 
finding is established within the scope of the reviewed public material.

## What contributions are welcome

1. **Identification of additional public NASA documents** relevant to the 
   MSM–VSM interface, formal verification, or safety assurance.
2. **Corrections to requirement identifiers** (e.g., L2-GW-0199, 
   L3-GLS-2001) if any are cited incorrectly.
3. **Citation corrections** — if you find a missing or incorrect reference.
4. **Typographical corrections** and minor clarifications.
5. **Suggestions for strengthening the traceability analysis** without 
   altering the central finding.

## What contributions are NOT accepted

1. Claims that NASA lacks a mechanism in implementation. The paper 
   explicitly limits its scope to public documentation.
2. Speculative claims about internal NASA artifacts not publicly available.
3. Reinterpretation of the central finding as a claim of implementation 
   deficiency.
4. Unsupported assertions about Gateway operational status.

## How to Contribute

1. Open an issue describing the contribution.
2. Reference the relevant section of the manuscript 
   (`paper/NASA_Gateway_Safety_Assurance_and_Formal_Verification.md`).
3. Submit a pull request.
4. Wait for review.

## Code of Conduct

Be precise. Distinguish between "not documented publicly" and "not 
implemented." The central methodological virtue of this study is that 
it maintains this distinction consistently.
