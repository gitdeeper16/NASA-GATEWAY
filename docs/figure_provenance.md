## Purpose

This document records the design intent and provenance of each 
figure used in the manuscript, to ensure transparency about 
what each figure shows and what it does not show.

## Figure 1 — Traceability Chain Gap

**Filename:** `figures/Fig1_Traceability_Chain_Gap.png`

**Content:** A vertical eight-step flowchart from hazard 
identification to verification evidence, with the 
Decision/Escalation Rule stage highlighted in red to mark 
where public documentation does not establish a complete trace.

**Design intent:** Visually summarize the analytical finding 
that public documentation establishes steps 1–4 partially, does 
not establish step 5, and only partially establishes steps 6–8.

**What it does NOT show:** It does not show that NASA lacks the 
mechanism at step 5; only that public documents do not establish it.

## Figure 2 — Two Pathways Interface

**Filename:** `figures/Fig2_Two_Pathways_Interface.png`

**Content:** Two parallel vertical columns (safety assurance 
on the left, formal verification on the right) converging at 
a red dashed box labeled "Unresolved Interface."

**Design intent:** Illustrate that the two pathways are 
independently documented but their connection point remains 
undemonstrated in public material.

## Figure 3 — MSM–VSM Architecture

**Filename:** `figures/Fig3_MSM_VSM_Architecture.png`

**Content:** Hierarchical diagram showing Ground ↔ VSM ↔ 
multiple MSMs, with AGC contracts as dashed lines and R2U2 
runtime monitoring attached to the VSM.

**Design intent:** Provide the architectural context for the 
traceability analysis.

## Figure 4 — Evidence Classification Taxonomy

**Filename:** `figures/Fig4_Evidence_Classification_Taxonomy.png`

**Content:** Six evidence categories arranged radially around 
a central hub.

**Design intent:** Illustrate the six-category classification 
used in Section 3.4 of the manuscript.

## Figure 5 — Gateway Lunar Orbit

**Filename:** `figures/Fig5_Gateway_Lunar_Orbit.png`

**Content:** Contextual illustration of Gateway in lunar orbit.

**Design intent:** Provide visual context in the introduction.

**Important:** This is an illustrative figure, not a technical 
diagram. It does not represent a specific configuration.

## Figure 6 — Integrated Assurance Case Structure (GSN)

**Filename:** `figures/Fig6_Assurance_Case_GSN_Structure.png`

**Content:** A Goal Structuring Notation (GSN) diagram showing 
how safety requirements, formal verification, and runtime 
monitoring must connect through a complete assurance argument.

**Design intent:** Propose a structure for what a complete 
traceability chain would look like, as a methodological 
contribution.
