# R4_control (parallel to R4): escalate if local_action_count >= 4
# R5_control (parallel to R5): escalate if comm_silence_cycles >= 3

# These thresholds are arbitrary. That is the point: they are fixed,
# documented, and not traceable to any safety constraint.
